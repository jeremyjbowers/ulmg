Game

A season has four stretches of games: the first half at home, the first half on the road, and the same again after the break. Between the halves there is a draft and a trading period, and no games. That is when rosters are set again. A player taken in the midseason Open draft has to be on the roster for the whole second half.

The home roster is the roster this half opened with, and the lineups filed from it. The other managers play your home games, and you cannot change who is on that roster. A call-up or a send-down changes the away roster only, which you play yourself. Usage counts in every game, home and away, so the computer will not use a player who is out of plate appearances or innings, and the home roster stays as it opened.
